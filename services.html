Color matching services for the Denver Metro Area and all of Colorado

From a single hard-to-match color to ongoing commercial supply, we match paint, powder coat, and finishes by hand and eye — beyond what a color computer can do on its own. Our matched paint is also what homeowners and professionals use for touch-up and repair.

Custom color matching

A color scan gets you a starting formula. We take it from there, adjusting by hand and by eye to finish the matches a scan alone can't. That means colors that are faded, rare, or just hard to get right.

  • Paint, powder coat, and other finish work
  • Hand and eye adjustment on top of computer-matched formulas
  • Paint matching for difficult and rare colors
  • Custom matched paint for touch-up, repair, and finish work

Touch-up paint & repair supply

We don't do the repair work ourselves — we supply the paint that makes it blend in. Our custom matched paint is used by homeowners touching up their own items and by professionals doing paint repair and surface repair for their customers.

  • Touch up paint matched to your existing finish, for metal, wood, vinyl, tile, and coated surfaces
  • Surface matching, so a touch-up or repair blends in with what's around it
  • Paint and coatings for professionals who do paint repair, surface repair, and surface touch-up
  • Stop by, or send us a message with the color you need to match
